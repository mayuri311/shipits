Executive Summary

Osprey@CMU centralizes your organization’s projects, discussions, and knowledge in a single, secure platform. It preserves context across officer transitions, boosts recruiting with a living portfolio of work, and reduces coordination overhead through smart discussion summaries and structured updates.

What It Solves

  • Fragmented tools: Replace scattered chats, docs, and sheets with one hub.
  • Leadership churn: Preserve institutional knowledge across semesters.
  • Visibility gap: Showcase outcomes to members, sponsors, and campus partners.
  • Coordination drag: Keep projects moving with updates, threads, and AI recaps.

Key Capabilities

Project Showcase

Rich pages with tags, media, collaborators, updates, and share tracking.

Discussions

Threaded comments, reactions, mentions, and markdown support.

AI Summaries

One‑click, context‑aware recaps of long threads and project updates.

Curated Lists

Share resource lists (VCs, sponsors, tools, venues) with contributors and analytics.

Real‑Time Chat

DMs and group chats with typing indicators for committees and teams.

Discovery

Search, autocomplete, “did you mean,” categories, featured, trending, and recommendations.

Notifications

Follows, likes, comment alerts; record shares across social platforms.

Events

Support for showcases, workshops, and meetups to drive engagement.

Outcomes for Student Orgs

  • More shipped projects: Clear pipelines and easier feedback loops.
  • Stronger recruiting: Candidates see a live portfolio of impact.
  • Better handoffs: New leaders ramp quickly with preserved context.
  • Sponsor‑ready: Shareable links and metrics that demonstrate outcomes.
